# Save & Restore Settings

`P`{.badge .text-bg-warning .prem-font} *This feature requires a one-time purchase on Google Play or the App Store. It is fully free on F-Droid and self-compiled builds.*

You spent twenty minutes getting the scroll speed and font size exactly right and now you don't want to do that again on a different device. This saves all of that.

![Settings storage in action](/images/features/settings-storage.webp){.w-100}

Save your settings to profiles and export them to a file, put it somewhere, import it later. Works for backups too if you're the kind of person who worries about that (I am).

You can even have multiple settings profiles --- perfect for when you need to swap speakers with different preferences quickly!
