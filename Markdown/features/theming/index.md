# Prompter Theming

A white-on-black prompter is fine until you're on a set where it reflects badly off the glass, or the director wants the talent looking at something that doesn't glare. So pick your own colors.

![Prompter theming in action](/images/features/theming.webp){.w-100}

Any background color, any text color --- match it to your set, cut glare on a beam-splitter, or just use the combination that's easiest on your eyes. And it's not only the prompter: the app itself has a theme and accent color too, so the whole thing looks the way you want it to.

There's no single "correct" prompter look, so TiefPrompt doesn't pretend there is one.
