# Voice Activation

`P`{.badge .text-bg-warning .prem-font} *This feature requires a one-time purchase on Google Play or the App Store. It is fully free on F-Droid and self-compiled builds.*

Elevate your scrolling text experience to the next level! With voice activation, the app only scrolls when you speak, providing a hands-free and seamless reading experience.
