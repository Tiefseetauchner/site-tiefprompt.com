# Mirror Modes

If you're running a proper teleprompter rig with a beam-splitter glass, the text comes out backwards. That's just how the optics work. TiefPrompt flips it back for you.

![Mirror modes in action](/images/features/mirror-modes.webp){.w-100}

Flip horizontally for true-prompter glass, flip vertically for mirrored hardware, or both at once if your setup is doing something unusual. Whatever your rig throws at the optics, the reader still reads it the right way round.

No external software, no weird workarounds --- just toggle the mirror that matches your hardware and you're prompting.
