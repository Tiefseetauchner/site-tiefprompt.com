# Current Chapter Headings

`P`{.badge .text-bg-warning .prem-font} *This feature requires a one-time purchase on Google Play or the App Store. It is fully free on F-Droid and self-compiled builds.*

Keep the most recent chapter visible! With Current Chapter Headings, you are able to display the most recent markdown heading and jump to it with a click.

![Current Chapter Heading Visible on the Prompter](/images/features/MarkdownAndChapter.webp){.w-100}

This way, you can always refer to the most recent chapter or simply have a visual queue for where in your script you are.
