# Reading Indicators

`P`{.badge .text-bg-warning .prem-font} *This feature requires a one-time purchase on Google Play or the App Store. It is fully free on F-Droid and self-compiled builds.*

When the text is scrolling it's surprisingly easy to lose track of which line you're actually on. Reading indicators put a bar above and below the active line so your eyes always have somewhere to land.

![Reading indicators in action](/images/features/reading-indicators.webp){.w-100}

The bars are adjustable in height, so you can frame a single line tightly or give yourself a wider band to read within --- whatever matches your reading pace and how far you're sitting from the screen.

It's a small thing, but once you've prompted with it you really don't want to go back.
